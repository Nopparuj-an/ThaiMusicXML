## Project Vision

ThaiMusicXML is an open, royalty-free digital notation standard built specifically for Thai traditional music (เพลงไทยเดิม/Phleng Thai Doem).

Because Western music theory (5-line staves, clefs, 12-TET divisions, sharps/flats, variable time signatures) does not natively map to Thai music, this format models native Thai primitives—7-tone scales, 8-measure grid systems, and structural speed levels (ชั้น/Chan). Thai traditional notation relies on a distinct conceptual architecture:

- 7-Tone System: Represented symbolically via solfège (ด, ร, ม, ฟ, ซ, ล, ท) or digits (1–7)
- Octave Markers: Represented via explicit attributes (low, middle, high) or native dot symbols (ดฺ, ดํ)

## Technical Inspiration & Acknowledgments

### MusicXML

Although ThaiMusicXML does not use or fork code from MusicXML, this project owes a major debt of inspiration to the [MusicXML standard](https://www.w3.org/2021/06/musicxml40/) maintained by the W3C Music Notation Community Group.

MusicXML proved to the world that an open, text-based XML standard could bridge the gap between incompatible music software, preserving cultural heritage and empowering independent developers. ThaiMusicXML adopts MusicXML's philosophy of open documentation, vendor neutrality, and community-driven governance to bring those same benefits to Thai music tradition.

### The Thai Music Tradition

We express our deepest respect and gratitude to the masters, scholars, and musicians who have preserved Phleng Thai Doem across generations. This digital standard is built to serve their legacy by making Thai musical heritage accessible to modern software developers, researchers, and students worldwide.
