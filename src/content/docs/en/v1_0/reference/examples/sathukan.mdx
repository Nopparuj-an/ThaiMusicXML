## Notes

สาธุการ is a หน้าพาทย์ piece: traditionally cued live rather than played to a
fixed repeat count. The source notation (a two-row ฆ้องวงใหญ่ transcription,
right hand over left) marks three points where the performance branches:
where the second time through starts, how far it plays before turning back,
and where it closes after returning to the top, worded as marginal notes
rather than as a fixed structure.

This score keeps that shape rather than forcing it into
[`<repeat>`](/en/v1_0/reference/elements/repeat/) and
[`<ending>`](/en/v1_0/reference/elements/ending/): each branch point is a
section boundary, with the original cue carried over verbatim as an
[`<annotation>`](/en/v1_0/reference/elements/annotation/) between sections.
A reader gets the same instruction a performer reading the source would.

Covers:

- Four [`<section>`](/en/v1_0/reference/elements/section/)s split exactly at
  the source's cue points, with `<annotation>` carrying the cues themselves
  rather than an encoded repeat
- `<part>`'s `stack` and `row` attributes joining ฆ้องวงใหญ่ R and L as the
  two rows of one instrument, the same pattern as
  [Khaek Borathes](/en/v1_0/reference/examples/khaek-borathes/)
- A long score with no Thai octave modifiers at all: every
  [`<note>`](/en/v1_0/reference/elements/note/) sits in one register, which
  the source notation does too
- [`<rest>`](/en/v1_0/reference/elements/rest/) throughout, with no `<chan>`
  or `<bpm>` set: the source gives no fixed tempo or ชั้น to record
