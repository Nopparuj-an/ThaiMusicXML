## Notes

ท่อน 2 is written out twice in the source, "รอบที่ 1" and "รอบที่ 2",
identical measure for measure except the very last one. That is exactly what
[`<repeat>`](/en/v1_0/reference/elements/repeat/) and
[`<ending>`](/en/v1_0/reference/elements/ending/) are for: the section is
written once and wrapped in `<repeat times="2">`, and only the differing
final line is repeated, inside an `<ending pass="2">`.

Covers:

- [`<repeat times="2">`](/en/v1_0/reference/elements/repeat/) wrapping a
  section, with [`<ending pass="2">`](/en/v1_0/reference/elements/ending/)
  substituting just its last line
- [`<parenthesis>`](/en/v1_0/reference/elements/parenthesis/) spans with
  `dim="true"`, marking four bracketed passages from the source as cued
  rather than played
- [`<group>`](/en/v1_0/reference/elements/group/) subdividing a beat into
  two in one measure and into three in another, inside a score that is
  otherwise a plain four notes per measure throughout
- An empty [`<instrument-name>`](/en/v1_0/reference/elements/instrument-name/)
  for a single melodic line the source does not attribute to any instrument
