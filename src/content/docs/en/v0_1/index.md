## Abstract

ThaiMusicXML is an open XML format for Thai traditional music (เพลงไทยเดิม/Phleng Thai Doem). One document holds one arrangement: its title and tuning, the ท่อน it is built from, the instruments in the ensemble, and what each of them plays, laid out in the same grid a musician reads on paper.

Version 0.1 covers pitched parts, unpitched parts such as ฉิ่ง, and lyric parts; ท่อน with repeats and endings; octave dots, subdivided beats, bow and link curves, cued passages, and the markings an arranger writes on the page. A RELAX NG schema defines the document, and the rules a grammar cannot state are written as conformance prose on the element pages.
